### 1. Central Mumbai Monsoon Waterlogging (2019)

- **Location**: Hindmata Junction, Dadar, Mumbai, India
- **Context**: Photojournalist capture of urban waterlogging widely recirculated in subsequent years as "Pune floods" or "Delhi floods".
